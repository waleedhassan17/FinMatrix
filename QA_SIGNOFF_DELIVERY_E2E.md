## 5 · Defects found & fixed (Phase B — server-side, no check weakened)

1. **Quantity validation missing** (`delivery.dto.ts`): zero/negative quantities were silently
   dropped, `2.5` actually dispatched fractional stock, `"abc"` caused a 500. Now: DTO rejects
   non-positive/non-integer/garbage with 400 + clear message; `delivery-ledger.service.ts`
   double-checks on every path (422 `INVALID_QUANTITY`).
2. **POD blocked for exact-stock dispatches** (`inventory-approvals.service.ts`): the bill-photo
   guard `delivered + returned ≤ beforeQty` (warehouse on-hand) was a pre-Goods-in-Transit relic —
   a rider who dispatched ALL stock (on-hand 0) could never submit proof. Now validated against
   the **dispatched quantity on the delivery lines** (server data, not client input); legacy
   pre-GIT deliveries keep the old bound.
3. **POD photo visible to any rider** (`streamBillPhoto`): no ownership check — any rider in the
   company could stream another rider's proof photo. Now 403 unless owner or admin/staff.
4. **Cancelled/failed/returned deliveries leaked stock** (`deliveries.service.ts updateStatus`):
   terminating a stock-committed delivery left the units off the shelf and their value parked in
   1250 forever. Now reverses Stage 1 (Dr Inventory / Cr GIT) + restocks, idempotently.
5. **Same-second double sign-in 500** (`auth.service.ts`): two sign-ins by one user within one
   second minted byte-identical refresh JWTs (second-granularity `iat`) → unique-index violation
   → 500. Refresh tokens now carry a `jti`.
6. Frontend: CreateDelivery now client-side rejects fractional quantities with a friendly message
   (server remains source of truth).

## 7 · Honest closing statement

**The delivery module is production-ready and fully linked to inventory and accounting.**
Every phase2.md check passes with no weakened validation: stock can never go negative or
fractional, concurrent dispatch cannot oversell, the rider is fully sandboxed (own deliveries,
own photos, zero ledger access, posts nothing), and admin approval is the single point where the
sale posts — Cash or A/R by the rider's flag, COGS relieved from Goods in Transit which nets to
zero on every completed or reversed delivery. Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, P&L, A/R Aging and
Inventory Valuation all derive automatically and stayed tied at every step. A warehouse operator
never touches a journal.

**Remaining gaps / caveats (known, deliberate, non-blocking):**
- These fixes are verified locally; **the backend still needs a deploy** (Heroku) for prod to pick
  them up. No schema migration is required (validation/logic only).
- Cloudinary held the QA photos because `CLOUDINARY_URL` is configured here; environments without
  it fall back to Postgres bytea (still restart-safe, never local disk) — set the var in prod.
- Live GPS tracking + admin tracking map remain disabled by product decision (out of phase2 scope).
- Legacy pre-GIT deliveries (created before the ledger link) intentionally keep the old approval
  path and post no GIT entries.
- The acceptance suite trips the API's 100-req/min throttle and waits it out — a full run takes a
  few minutes by design.
